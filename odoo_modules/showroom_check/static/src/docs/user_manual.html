What this app is for

The showroom should look a certain way, and somebody has already photographed each view of it as it should look. Your job is to show the room as it is today.

You do not judge anything yourself. Capture the room honestly and the server does the comparing.

Signing in

You only do this once. Type the address your manager gave you into Server address — you do not need to press anything, the app checks it on its own and shows a green tick. Pick your Database if a list appears, then fill in Username and Password and tap Sign in.

The sign-in screen, with the server connected.

The Dashboard

This is the screen you will live on. The ring shows how many of today's rounds are done. The large card tells you what matters right now: Open now when a round is ready, or Up next with a countdown when it is not.

  • Open now — tap Capture now and go.
  • Upcoming — nothing to do yet.
  • Missed — the window closed with nothing captured.
  • Recorded — done. A Watch button appears.

The Dashboard, with the progress ring and today's rounds.

Capturing a round

Tap Capture now. If your workplace allows both, a Photos and Video switch appears. Either is checked the same way — pick whichever suits the room.

Video

Tap Record now and turn slowly on the spot, letting the camera pass across every part of the room. The view names tick off along the bottom as you pass them.

Stop does not always stop. On most phones it pauses, and Finish now is what ends the clip.

Checking the clip before you send it

When the sweep ends you are not finished. The app plays the clip back under Check the recording, with the view names running along the bottom that light up as the playback reaches each one — so you can see which walls the sweep actually covered.

Then decide: Use it sends the round, Record again throws the clip away and starts over.

This is the moment to catch a sweep that pointed at the floor or missed a wall. Once a round is sent it is filed for the day, and only a manager can delete it so it can be walked again.

Photographs

The app walks you through the views one at a time, showing Step 1/4 at the top. Tap How it should look to see the original. Take the picture, then Use it to keep it or Retake photograph to try again.

Nothing is saved until Sending… finishes. There is no draft and no resume — if you leave part way through, the round has to be done again from the start.

Reading the result

Once a round is captured, Watch on its card plays the clip back and shows each view beside its original.

  • As expected — the view matches.
  • Worth a look — a little different.
  • Something has changed — clearly different.
  • Could not line up — the two pictures could not be matched, usually because the framing was too different.

Similar is how much of the same thing is in both pictures. Match is whether anything actually moved.

A low score is not an accusation. It means the two pictures differ — which can be a real change, or simply that you stood somewhere different.

Language, help and logging out

Tap Profile at the bottom. Tap EN or عربي to switch the whole app between English and Arabic. Help opens the guides your workplace has published.

The Profile tab, with the language switch and Help.

Worth remembering

  • A round can only be captured inside its window, and only once a day.
  • There are no reminders. Open the app to see whether a round is waiting.
  • The app needs a signal for everything — there is no offline mode.
  • No sound is ever recorded.
  • Times shown are the office's timezone, not your phone's.
